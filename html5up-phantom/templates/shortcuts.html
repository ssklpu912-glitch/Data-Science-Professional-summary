Keyboard shortcuts & tips

LabLogbook doesn't define custom keybindings yet — these are your browser's built-in shortcuts applied to this app, plus a few workflow tips.

Browser shortcuts that help here

  • Tab / Shift+Tab — move between fields on a long form (Experiments, Characterizations, Elements) without reaching for the mouse.
  • Ctrl/Cmd+F — search within a long results page, like Full log (date-wise) or a paper search results list.
  • Ctrl/Cmd+Click a link — opens it in a new tab, useful for comparing two technique workspaces or two paper analyses side by side.
  • Alt/Option+Left (or Backspace on some setups) — browser back, faster than clicking "← Back to technique categories" every time.

Workflow tips

  • On Experiments, use the "compare" picker on two past entries to get an automatic diff of status, input parameters, and results.
  • On Elements, "Clone for an experiment" copies a protocol's latest version and links it to the experiment you pick — the original protocol and its history are untouched.
  • Search results on Search Papers keep your recent searches — click into the search box to see them again.
  • Tagging uploaded files by channel/type in a technique's Select data tab is what makes them show up correctly in the technique-specific tabs (Topography, Mechanical, etc.).
