Documentation

A short guide to each part of LabLogbook.

Experiments

Log a new experiment from the Experiments page: date/time, title, type, status, objective, materials, procedure, input parameters, output/results, observations, and conclusion. Past entries appear below the form — pick any two to compare side by side, and LabLogbook will point out what changed between them (status, input parameters, output).

Characterizations & Data Interpretation

Under Data Interpretation, pick a technique category (Spectroscopy, Microscopy & Imaging, etc.), then a specific technique to open its workspace. Each technique workspace is organized into tabs down the left side:

  • Select data — upload or choose the files you're working with for this technique.
  • Technique-specific tabs (e.g. Topography, Mechanical, SAED for AFM/TEM) — measure, fit, and annotate the selected data.
  • Analysis — review everything you've measured for the current file selection.

General plotting/formatting of tabular or image data (without a specific technique workflow) lives under Characterizations → Data Interpretation Workspace: select files, plot, format, take a derivative, then analyze.

Research Papers

Use Search Papers to look up literature by topic (Semantic Scholar, CrossRef, and arXiv). For any result, Add to log saves it straight to your collection, or Analyze / Summarize pulls out key points from the abstract (and the open-access PDF, when one exists) before you save it. Everything you've saved is listed on the Miscellaneous (Research Papers) page, grouped by journal.

Elements — Protocols & Projects

The Elements page has two tabs:

  • Protocol & SOP Repository — write a reusable procedure once, then create new versions as it evolves (each version keeps its own change note and full step list) or clone it for a specific experiment.
  • Project & Task Management — track a project's timeline, grant/funding period, and milestones/deliverables, plus a to-do list that can optionally link a task to a specific experiment.
