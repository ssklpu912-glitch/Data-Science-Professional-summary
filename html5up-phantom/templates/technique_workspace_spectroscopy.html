The classic workflow this mirrors: run a blank, then a multi-level standard series per compound (building its own calibration curve), identify each compound's retention time, then run the sample and match its peaks against those retention times. A peak is only named if its RT falls within 0.2 min of a library entry — there's no compound database, so this is only as good as the reference data you bring from your own column and conditions. Give a compound 2+ lines with concentration and peak area to fit its own calibration curve; matched sample peaks are then quantified with that compound's curve automatically. Peaks are integrated valley-to-valley; N (plates) uses the half-height-width formula; tailing factor is the USP 5%-height asymmetry; resolution is an approximation using half-height widths rather than base widths.
